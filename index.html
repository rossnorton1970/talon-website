why talon

the problem

agents are powerful, but they don't learn. they don't remember. they fail constantly, and they need constant hand-holding. most agent platforms make you write endless skill files and manage memory manually. it's slow, it's fragile, and it doesn't scale.

the solution

talon is built on six complete phases of intelligence. from three-tier memory that never forgets, to five-level failure recovery, to security hardening that's built in, not bolted on. deploy once. your agents improve with every interaction. they remember. they scale.

six layers that compound

each layer builds on the last. agents don't just follow instructions — they learn, remember, and recover from failure intelligently.

memory that works

three-tier memory architecture. agents remember your business rules, customer context, and everything they've learned. semantic storage means they find what matters, not just what matches keywords.

routing that thinks

llm coordinator decides which model to use, based on task complexity and sensitivity. budget tracking prevents runaway costs. telegram integration means agents work where your team works.

skill generation

agents reflect on their work and create new skills automatically. no more writing skill files. your agents get smarter the longer they run — 40% token cost reduction observed in production.

governance at scale

kanban boards for multi-agent coordination. checkpoints and state recovery. zombie detection. approval workflows. grow your agent fleet without losing control.

owner experience

your customers get their own project workspace. one-time setup links. onboarding in minutes, not weeks. agents that understand the context, not just the commands.

resilience & safety

goal locking. five-level failure escalation. privacy router (keeps sensitive data local). prompt injection detection. popia and gdpr compliance built in from day one.
